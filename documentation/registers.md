- **Consumed by** — Report §3.3, Table 5; Tech §9.6
- **Status** — **VERIFIED**: monotone to the no-intercooler limit, which is the recuperated baseline

  Caveats recorded with the result:

  - Best 36.453 % at 7.90 MPa against the recuperated baseline's 36.541 %.
  - The geometric-mean rule is an ideal-gas result and sCO2 is not an ideal gas here, so it is checked rather than assumed.
  - Intercooling reduces compressor work as intended; it loses overall because the heat dumped in the intercooler is heat the recuperator would have recovered.
  - The intermediate pressure that minimises back-work ratio is not the one that maximises efficiency. The recuperator penalty, not the compression saving, sets the optimum.
  - Efficiency improves monotonically as the intermediate pressure approaches the compressor inlet, i.e. as the intercooler is switched off. The conclusion is not that the intermediate pressure was badly chosen; it is that the layout does not pay here.

- **Outputs** — `results/tolerance_sensitivity/sweep.csv` (16 rows), `results/tolerance_sensitivity/mass_propagation.csv` (5 rows), `results/tolerance_sensitivity/knee_stability.csv` (3 rows)
- **Manifest** — `results/tolerance_sensitivity/manifest.json`, written 2026-11-06T19:15:22Z
- **Source fingerprint** — `65f1e4b8ee4f7fb1` over 73 files
- **Consumed by** — Report §8.2, App A.2; Tech §9.11
- **Status** — **VERIFIED**: 9.6e-6 points across all settings; same knee at 25/100/400 nodes

  Caveats recorded with the result:

  - Largest deviation 9.55e-06 efficiency points and 0.041 % UA across all settings.
  - Efficiency and UA do not converge at the same rate. The pinch is a minimum, so its first-order discretisation error vanishes near the extremum; UA is an integral of dQ/dT and its does not. Resolution therefore moves the mass estimate while leaving the efficiency untouched.
  - A loose tear tolerance (1e-6) is rejected by the exergy balance, not the first-law closure. The second-law check is the more sensitive convergence detector of the two, which is worth knowing when diagnosing a cycle that 'converged' but looks wrong.
  - This tests numerical convergence only. Whether the converged answer is physically right is what the literature validation establishes, and the two prove different things.
  - relax changes the path to the answer, not the answer. Any sensitivity to it would mean the tear loop is stopping early.
  - energy_tol is a diagnostic gate and must not change any number; it is swept to confirm that.

## 2. Validation register

*Validated* means agreement with an independent reference.
*Tested* means an automated check passes. *Verified* means the
arithmetic is right. *Unvalidated* means implemented with
nothing external to check against — a statement of what is
known, not a criticism.

| What | Status | Evidence | Where | What it does *not* show |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Solver numerics | **VALIDATED** | Air-standard Brayton vs the closed form, 4.6e-16 | `tests/test_solver.py` | The block equations, passes and tear are right. Says nothing about CO2. |
| CO2 properties | **VALIDATED** | Dostal's state table, 0.002 % on enthalpy | `tests/test_validation.py` | The equation of state as used here, at these states. Three entropy disagreements were traced to errors in his table. |
| Whole cycle | **VALIDATED** | Kim (2018): 44.669 % vs 44.670 %, and his optimum found independently in two directions | `tests/test_validation.py` | The strongest evidence here. Matching an optimum means the model's derivatives are right, not just one value. |

## 4. Known limitations

Carried from the validation register and the studies' own
recorded caveats.

- Mass, cost, payback and the selection rest on the brief's
  illustrative coefficients. Efficiency and margin do not.
- Exergy totals exclude the heater, which has no boundary
  temperature in the model. Layouts stay comparable; absolute
  totals are partial.
- Every efficiency is a design-point number. Off-design and
  part-load are not modelled.
- Turbomachinery mass is held constant across layouts.
- Pressure drops are stated, not derived from geometry.
- The 7.7 MPa inlet constraint admits two readings and they
  select different layouts. The literal reading is used.
- Partial cooling was swept one variable at a time, not
  jointly over exhaust pressure and split fraction.
