# S2: compressor-inlet temperature drift (off-design proxy)

For each named design the recuperators are frozen at their design absolute UA (UAperFlow, iterated until the mass flow settles), pressures and split are fixed, heat input stays 100 MWth, and T_in is moved from 32.0 to 36.0 C in 0.25 K steps (both coolers deliver T_in).

**Cooler:** the solver's cooler accepts only a pinch spec and its CO2 outlet is pinned at T_in, so the coolers could not be frozen at a UA. They keep the 5 K pinch spec (so the cooler pinch is 5 K by construction) and the cooler UA they *would* need is reported instead.

**Limitations:** fixed isentropic efficiencies, no compressor or turbine maps, fixed pressures and split, no control action. This is a sensitivity estimate, not a true off-design calculation. Points whose recuperator pinch falls below 5 K are kept and flagged.
